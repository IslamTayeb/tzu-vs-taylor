    How it works

    1. Collect. lines: verses from Sun Tzu's The Art of War (Lionel Giles, 1910) and Taylor Swift lyrics from reputation plus a few songs and interviews.
    2. Turn text into numbers. A pre-trained AI (MiniLM) reads each line and squeezes its meaning into 384 numbers, called an embedding. Lines that mean similar things get similar numbers.
    3. Learn. A simple model learns which patterns in those numbers mean Taylor and which mean Sun Tzu.
    4. Test. We hid lines while it learned. It got of them right.
