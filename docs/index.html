    How it works

    1. Collect. lines: verses from Sun Tzu's The Art of War (Lionel Giles, 1910) and Taylor Swift lyrics from reputation plus a few songs and interviews.
    2. Turn text into numbers, two ways. The word counter gives each line one number per word or word pair, with rare words counting for more. The meaning model uses a pre-trained AI (MiniLM) that squeezes the meaning of a line into 384 numbers, called an embedding.
    3. Learn. Each model learns weights that push those numbers toward Taylor or toward Sun Tzu.
    4. Test. We hid lines while they learned. The word counter got right; the meaning model got .
